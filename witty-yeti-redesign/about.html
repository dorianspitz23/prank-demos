Who we are

We make the pranks
you'd send yourself — if you could.

Witty Yeti is a small team in Boulder, Colorado, shipping gag gifts that look alarmingly legitimate. The joke doesn't need a punchline. The packaging does the work.

Why we exist

A good prank used to be something you made yourself

Back when we were kids, a prank meant a Saran-wrapped toilet seat, a rubber band on the kitchen sprayer, or a note stuffed in a cousin's shoe. It was tactile. It took effort. It looked real — because it was.

Somewhere along the way, pranks got reduced to a dollar-store aisle of plastic junk: spring-loaded snakes, joy buzzers, whoopee cushions that sound nothing like the thing they're imitating. We make pranks for people who remember when it was better.

How we do it

Real factories. Real steel. Real shrink-wrap.

Every product in our catalog was designed from scratch and manufactured — not white-labeled. The Dehydrated Water can is 16 ounces of real steel with a shrink-wrapped label that scans at a grocery register. The Tactical Shart Kit is an actual tin box with printed inserts. The parking cards are four-color offset printed on card stock.

This matters because the prank only works when the packaging doesn't. Plastic novelty junk reveals itself instantly. Our stuff gets filed into a cupboard for three weeks before the recipient notices what they're holding.

Our rule

Humor that doesn't punch down

Nobody is called a target here. Nobody is called a victim. We don't sell revenge. We don't sell humiliation. If a prank only works because the recipient is a "loser" or a "wife" or a "roommate who sucks," we don't make it.

The test every product passes: would the recipient laugh when they find out it was you? If the answer is no, we didn't make it. We sell pranks for the people you actually love.
