Ordering & pricing

What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and Shop Pay. Crypto: no. Venmo from your friend who owes you money: also no.
Will the charge say "Witty Yeti" on my card statement?
It will — which matters because the recipient never sees your card statement, only the package. If you share a card with someone who'd be suspicious of the charge, use a gift card or PayPal.
Can I use multiple discount codes?
One code per order. Bundle discounts apply automatically and stack with one promo code — so signing up for our newsletter while also ordering a bundle gets you both.
Do you do bulk / wholesale orders?
Yes — email hi@wittyyeti.com with quantity and we'll send a wholesale sheet. Minimum order is 24 units. Popular with bachelorette parties, corporate "team-building," and one very specific wedding officiant.
Why is the Dehydrated Water $10.99 and not $1?
Because it's a real 16oz steel can with a real shrink-wrapped label that scans at a register. The joke works because the packaging doesn't. A $1 joke would look like a $1 joke.

Shipping & delivery

How fast does it ship?
In-stock items leave the warehouse within one business day. Continental US orders arrive in 3–5 business days. Alaska/Hawaii: 5–7. International: 10–14.
Is shipping really free?
Yes. All US orders, no minimum, no gotchas. International shipping is calculated at checkout and varies by country.
What does the shipping label say?
A generic LLC name that does not say "Witty Yeti" anywhere. No return address. No packing slip with your name, email, or anything identifying. You are a ghost with a credit card.
Can I track the order?
Yes — you get a tracking number as soon as the label prints. Refresh obsessively, we won't judge.
Will you ship internationally?
We ship to Canada, UK, EU, Australia, and NZ. A few countries have customs issues with our packaging (notably: Germany. Good luck explaining Dehydrated Water to a customs officer). Full list at checkout.

The prank itself

Will the recipient know it's from me?
Only if you confess. There's no return address, no Witty Yeti logo, no packing slip, and no way for them to trace the package back to you unless you send yourself the same thing from the same address (don't).
Can I add a custom note?
Yes. 200 characters max. Popular: blank. "From a concerned neighbor." "Hydration is important." "Saw this and thought of you."
Is this mean? I feel weird.
Our rule: if the recipient would laugh when they find out it was you, you're fine. If they wouldn't — send them flowers instead. Pranks are for the people you actually love.
Can I actually drink the Dehydrated Water?
No. It's a steel can full of absolutely nothing. We say this on the label. Twice. Please don't drink the can.
Will the stickers damage the surface they're on?
Depends on the surface and how long they're there. Printed car paint: usually removes clean within 60 days. Older paint, rust, or heavy sun exposure: might peel a layer. We don't recommend sticking them on someone's Ferrari and then calling it a prank.

Returns & guarantee

What's the Yeti Guarantee?
60 days. If the package arrived dented, the sticker didn't stick, or the prank misfired for any reason — email hi@wittyyeti.com and we refund or replace. No proof-of-purchase theatre.
What if my friend hates it?
Historically unusual. But if it happens, the guarantee covers you. We'll refund the order — no questions about the relationship. We're not therapists.
Can I return it if I change my mind?
Unopened items: yes, within 30 days, minus original shipping. Opened items: same-day buyer's-remorse is usually fine if nothing's used; after that it's case-by-case.
What if I sent it to the wrong address?
Email us within 1 hour of order and we can usually still edit the label. After that, it's shipped — but we'll replace the order at cost so you don't waste the good-prank-on-the-wrong-mailbox energy.
Do you replace items lost in transit?
Yes. If tracking shows the package as "delivered" but the recipient didn't get it, email us and we'll send a replacement. (Porch pirates stealing Dehydrated Water — unintentionally the best prank we offer.)
