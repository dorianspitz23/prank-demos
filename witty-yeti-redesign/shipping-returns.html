Shipping & Returns

Last updated: April 17, 2026

Free shipping, every order

All US orders ship free — no minimum, no gotchas. That's not a limited-time promotion or a referral-only perk. It's just how it works.

How fast do we ship?

In-stock items leave our Boulder, CO warehouse within one business day. Orders placed before 2pm MT typically ship the same day.

  • Continental US: 3–5 business days after shipment
  • Alaska & Hawaii: 5–7 business days
  • Canada: 7–10 business days (calculated at checkout)
  • UK / EU / Australia / NZ: 10–14 business days (calculated at checkout)

What the recipient sees

Nothing identifies the package as coming from Witty Yeti. Specifically:

  • A generic LLC name appears on the label instead of "Witty Yeti"
  • No return address is included
  • There is no packing slip inside the box
  • The outer box has zero branding — plain corrugated cardboard
  • Email confirmations go to your email, not the recipient's

The only way the recipient knows the package is a prank is when they open it. That's the whole product.

Tracking

Every order gets a tracking number, emailed to you as soon as the label prints. Refresh it obsessively. That's normal. The best part of the experience is the wait.

The Witty Yeti Guarantee

Our guarantee is 60 days, no questions asked. That means:

  • If the package arrived damaged — we replace it, free
  • If the sticker doesn't stick — we send a replacement, free
  • If the recipient hated it and the relationship is collateral damage — we refund your order
  • If the shipping carrier lost the box — we re-ship or refund, your call

Email hi@wittyyeti.com with your order number. We respond within 24 hours (business days). No proof-of-purchase theatre. No "were you 100% sure you wanted to send this to your mother-in-law?" interrogation.

Returns (unopened items)

Unopened, undamaged items can be returned within 30 days for a refund minus original shipping cost. Email us first — we'll send a return label at your cost. Once we receive the item, refund goes back to your original payment method within 5 business days.

Returns (opened items)

Generally, no — the moment a prank product is opened, the prank is over, and we can't resell it. Exceptions: manufacturing defects, items damaged in transit, or obvious buyer's-remorse within a few hours of receipt (just message us, we're reasonable).

Changing or canceling an order

We ship fast, which is great for pranks but bad for second-guessing. If you need to change or cancel:

  • Within 1 hour of order: email us and we can usually still edit the label or cancel
  • After 1 hour: the label is printed. We can't stop the package, but we'll replace the order at cost if you misdirected it.
  • After shipment: once it's in the carrier's hands, it's going. You can refuse delivery and we'll refund once we get it back.

International customs

We ship to Canada, UK, EU (most countries), Australia, and New Zealand. A few countries occasionally flag our packaging at customs because "Dehydrated Water" reads as a weird import description. Canada and the UK: no issues historically. Germany: 50/50. If customs seizes a package, we'll refund the order.

Lost or stolen packages

If tracking shows "delivered" but the recipient didn't get the package, email us within 7 days and we'll replace it. (Porch pirates stealing Dehydrated Water is unintentionally the best prank we offer.)

Questions

Email hi@wittyyeti.com. We answer every message, usually within a few hours during business days. We're in Boulder, Colorado — so responses after 5pm MT might come the next morning.

The 60-Day Promise

Email us any time within 60 days of delivery. We'll replace it, refund it, or figure it out. That's it.
